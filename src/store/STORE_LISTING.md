# Google Play 商店文案草稿

**名稱（≤30 字）**：Hush Shield: Voice Privacy
**簡短說明（≤80 字）**：Blend your conversation into the room with fan, AC or café sounds.
**簡短說明（繁中）**：用風扇、冷氣或咖啡廳般的環境音，降低對話被聽清的程度。

**完整說明（草稿）**
Hush Shield plays a continuous masking sound — speech-shaped noise, a babble of synthetic voices, or a low-profile room tone (desk fan, air-conditioner, café murmur) — and listens through your microphone to raise the level slightly when voices appear.

• Four masking modes, adjustable level, 3-second fade-in
• Live spectrum and voice-activity meter
• Optional local notes: Wi-Fi networks in range and paired Bluetooth devices, saved only on your phone
• No account, no ads, no network upload

Please note: masking can reduce how clearly speech is captured by a nearby recorder, but it cannot guarantee that a conversation is not recorded or transcribed. The app cannot detect whether another device is recording. Use at a comfortable volume and in accordance with local law.

**類別**：工具 ／ 標籤：隱私、白噪音
**內容分級**：完成問卷（無暴力、無使用者互動內容）
**目標對象**：18 歲以上
**廣告**：無
