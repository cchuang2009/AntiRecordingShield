# Privacy Policy — Hush Shield

Last updated: <DATE>   Contact: <YOUR EMAIL>

**Microphone.** The app uses the microphone only while monitoring is running, to measure sound levels and voice-like activity. Audio is processed in memory on your device and is never recorded to storage, uploaded or shared.

**Wi-Fi and Bluetooth (optional).** If you press "Scan Wi-Fi" or "Paired Bluetooth", the app reads nearby Wi-Fi network names/identifiers or the list of Bluetooth devices already paired with your phone. Results are shown on screen and may be saved in the app's local history if you choose. Android requires the Location permission (and the system Location switch) before it returns Wi-Fi scan results; the app does not read, store or upload your position. Results are not uploaded or shared.

**Bluetooth scan (optional).** If you press "Start scan" in the Bluetooth radar, the app listens for nearby Bluetooth Low Energy advertisements and Classic Bluetooth devices and shows their names, addresses and signal strength with an estimated distance. This is shown on screen and may be kept in the app's local history if you choose; Android requires the Location permission (and the Location switch) for Bluetooth scanning too; the app does not read, store or upload your position. Results are not uploaded or shared.

**Stored data.** Event history, device labels and snapshots are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. You can delete history inside the app or by uninstalling the app.

**Network, ads, analytics.** The app does not request internet access, contains no advertising and no analytics SDKs.

**Children.** The app is not directed to children under 18.

**Changes.** We will update this page if the app's data practices change.
